# Tracking Cancer Through Text: Longitudinal Extraction From Radiology Reports Using Open-Source Large Language Models

**What it is and what it can establish:** A six-page report of a locally run qwen2.5-72b pipeline that extracts target, non-target and new lesions, with sizes, from pairs of Dutch CT reports, scored by two readers on 50 pairs. It can establish how accurately a prompted large open model extracts RECIST lesion data; it does not derive the overall response category.

**Limits:** Short preprint, 50 test pairs from one hospital, Dutch text, a 72B model (not small), no fine-tuning, no comparison with other models in the paper.

**Read for:** G-01.
